## Sclosa's Conjecture 1.3: what is refuted and what is true

Refuted (already for K_6): closed embedded pieces, finite or countable (the version-of-record wording); images of immersions of compact boundaryless manifolds; pieces closed only relative to the equilibrium set or the stable set.
True for every graph: locally closed smooth pieces (the literal arXiv v1 wording, which omits "closed"), by stratification; compact manifolds with boundary or corners, by triangulation.
